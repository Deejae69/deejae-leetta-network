## 2. BUSINESS DESCRIPTION

DeeJae LeEtta LLC is a multi-vertical technology and digital assets company operating at the intersection of blockchain infrastructure, decentralized finance, gaming, and sustainable energy. The company develops and manages the **DeeJae LeEtta Network** — an Ethereum-based business network — and issues the native utility token **D33J Coin**.

The LLC's operations span four core divisions:

### Division 1 — Blockchain & Cryptocurrency
- Development and management of the **DeeJae LeEtta Network** on Ethereum
- Issuance and governance of **D33J Coin** (ERC-20 utility token)
- Digital asset portfolio management
- Decentralized finance (DeFi) participation and yield strategies
- Wallet: Ethereum Wallet ...Ce00EE | Coinbase Wallet ...46b83d | Ethereum Wallet ...86052F

### Division 2 — Bitcoin Mining Operations
- Active Bitcoin mining via **Lucky Miner** hardware
- Mining Specifications:
  - Power Consumption: 2.15W
  - Input Voltage: 5,309 mV
  - Input Current: 404 mA
  - Frequency: 485 MHz
  - Measured Core Voltage: 1,216 mV
  - Fan Speed: 5,825 RPM
  - Chip Temperature: 43°C
  - Best Difficulty Achieved: 4.29G
- Private network mining (192.168.1.220 — Local)
- Expansion into home mining and decentralized mining collectives planned

### Division 3 — Solar Energy & Infrastructure (Nigeria)
- 75-acre Solar Farm development in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria
- Battery storage systems
- Data center infrastructure
- Vertical gardens and agrivoltaic integration
- Business registration in progress under Nigerian law

### Division 4 — MMO Gaming & Entertainment
- Development of a proprietary Massively Multiplayer Online (MMO) game
- In-game currency: **D33J Coin** (live Ethereum ERC-20 token)
- Wallet integration: Reown (WalletConnect) in-game wallet
- Play-to-earn model: players own assets, trade freely, build real wealth
